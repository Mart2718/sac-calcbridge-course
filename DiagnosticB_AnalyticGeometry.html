How to use this page

These problems cover the geometry of lines, circles, and regions — tools used constantly in calculus when writing tangent lines and analyzing graphs. Work on paper first, then reveal answers.

1
Find an equation for the line that passes through the point $(2, -5)$ and satisfies the given condition.
0/4 rated
▾
(a)
$\text{Has slope } -3$
Answer
$y = -3x + 1$
Point-slope: $y-(-5) = -3(x-2) \Rightarrow y = -3x+1$
(b)
$\text{Is parallel to the } x\text{-axis}$
Answer
$y = -5$
Horizontal line through $(2,-5)$
(c)
$\text{Is parallel to the } y\text{-axis}$
Answer
$x = 2$
Vertical line through $(2,-5)$
(d)
$\text{Is parallel to the line } 2x - 4y = 3$
Answer
$y = \tfrac{1}{2}x - 6$
Slope of given line: $y = \frac{1}{2}x - \frac{3}{4}$, so slope $= \frac{1}{2}$. Through $(2,-5)$: $y+5 = \frac{1}{2}(x-2)$

Find an equation for the circle that has center $(-1, 4)$ and passes through the point $(3, -2)$.
0/1 rated
▾
(a)
$\text{Write the equation}$
Answer
$(x+1)^2 + (y-4)^2 = 52$
Radius $= \sqrt{(3-(-1))^2+(-2-4)^2} = \sqrt{16+36} = \sqrt{52}$

4
Let $A(-7,\,4)$ and $B(5,\,-12)$ be points in the plane.
0/6 rated
▾
(a)
$\text{Find the slope of line } AB$
Answer
$-\dfrac{4}{3}$
$m = \dfrac{-12-4}{5-(-7)} = \dfrac{-16}{12} = -\dfrac{4}{3}$
(b)
$\text{Find the equation of line } AB \text{ and its intercepts}$
Answer
$4x+3y+16=0;\;\; x\text{-int}=-4,\; y\text{-int}=-\tfrac{16}{3}$
(c)
$\text{Find the midpoint of segment } AB$
Answer
$(-1,\,-4)$
(d)
$\text{Find the length of segment } AB$
Answer
$20$
$d = \sqrt{12^2+(-16)^2} = \sqrt{144+256} = \sqrt{400} = 20$
(e)
$\text{Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of } AB$
Answer
$3x - 4y = 13$
Perpendicular slope $= \frac{3}{4}$, through midpoint $(-1,-4)$
(f)
$\text{Find the equation of the circle with diameter } AB$
Answer
$(x+1)^2+(y+4)^2=100$
Center = midpoint $(-1,-4)$, radius $= \frac{20}{2} = 10$

Line Equations

(a)
Find the equation of the line perpendicular to $y = 3x - 1$ passing through $(-3,\,2)$.
Answer
$y = -\dfrac{1}{3}x + 1$
Perpendicular slope $= -\frac{1}{3}$. Point-slope: $y - 2 = -\frac{1}{3}(x+3) \Rightarrow y = -\frac{1}{3}x + 1$
S2
Circle Equations
0/1 rated
▾
(a)
Find the equation of the circle with center $(2,\,-3)$ that is tangent to the $x$-axis.
Answer
$(x-2)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 9$
Tangent to the $x$-axis means the radius equals the distance from the center to the $x$-axis: $r = |-3| = 3$.

Geometric Regions
0/1 rated
▾
(a)
Sketch the region defined by $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$ and $y > x$.
Answer
Half-disk: interior of disk of radius $3$ centered at origin, strictly above the line $y = x$. The circular arc (where $x^2+y^2=9$ and $y>x$) is included; the diameter along $y=x$ and both intersection points of the line and circle are excluded.
$x^2+y^2 \leq 9$ is the closed disk of radius 3. $y > x$ (strict inequality) cuts along $y=x$ — shade the upper-left portion. Draw the circular boundary as solid but the chord $y=x$ as dashed, with no filled points where they meet.

How this course works
1
Take one of the four Diagnostics
Each diagnostic is a short self-check (5–10 questions) on one skill area: Algebra, Analytic Geometry, Functions, or Trigonometry. You'll see exactly which topics you're solid on — and which ones are worth revisiting before Calculus 1.
2
Use the Diagnostic Lookup Tool
For any problem you missed, the Lookup Tool shows you two paths — choose what fits:
🔄 Quick Refresh
Practice problems with show/hide answers and hints. Good for "I was close — I just need to drill it."
📖 Deep Dive
Links to the MyOpenMath lesson for that exact skill. Good for "I need to actually learn this from scratch."
3
When you feel ready — take the Quick Check
Each skill area has a graded Quick Check inside this MyOpenMath course. When you've practiced enough to feel confident, go take it. These are the checkpoints your instructor can see, and each one unlocks a badge.
🏅
Earn your badge — repeat for each area
Pass the Quick Check (≥ 80%) and you earn that section's badge. Work through all four and you earn the CalcBridge Complete badge — your signal (and ours) that you're ready for Calculus 1.

Before you start
A few things worth knowing
  • You don't have to work through all four diagnostics in order. Start with the area you're least confident in — that's usually where the most growth is waiting.
  • The diagnostics are self-graded. Be honest with yourself — the data is for you, not a gradebook.
  • The Quick Checks in MyOpenMath are the only graded pieces. Everything else — the diagnostics, the Lookup Tool, the AI practice — is for your own preparation.
  • If a skill feels completely unfamiliar, go straight to the MyOpenMath lesson. The practice problems in the Lookup Tool are for review, not first-time learning.
  • The Play Lab AI is free for all SAC students and works best when you paste the prompt directly from the "AI Practice ↗" button in the Lookup Tool.

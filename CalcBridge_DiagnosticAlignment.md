# CalcBridge — Diagnostic-to-Course Alignment Map
**Updated:** November 2026 · Based on MyOpenMath Cartridge CCEXPORT327031 (November update)

**Purpose:** For each item on Diagnostics A–D, this map shows exactly where a student can find support inside the MyOpenMath course — in Foundational Fluency (Section 2) and/or Calculus Before Calculus (Section 3).

> **November 2026 Updates:** Added **E.8** (Systems of Nonlinear Inequalities) closing B5 gap; added **1.3B** (Trig Identities & Equations) with dedicated web apps closing D6/D7/D8 gaps; added **Unit L** (Exponential & Logarithmic Functions) as new college-algebra-level unit; **1.5** (Exp & Log at precalc level) confirmed in CBC. Quadratic material is **Unit F** throughout.

**Coverage key:** ✅ Fully covered | ⚠️ Partially covered | ❌ Gap — supplemented by CalcBridge app
